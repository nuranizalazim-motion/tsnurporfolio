about me

I'm Aniza, a senior lecturer in Creative Motion Design at the Faculty of Art & Design, UiTM Puncak Alam.

My work sits where motion graphics, illustration and interactive media meet. I research that meeting point too: motion illustration in Malaysian media, typography in documentary film, and how 3D, AR and VR help people learn.

I'm a registered Professional Technologist (Ts.) in Art, Design & Creative Multimedia Technology, and I'm in the final year of my PhD. I also build tools for my own teaching, like the FSSR 2026 dashboard, and characters like Miss Nur, who you met at the top of this page.
